# XolveManager Keyword Map

## Research limitations

No keyword-volume, difficulty, competitor, or trend data was collected. The phrases below are initial intent-based targets, not claims about search demand or expected ranking. Validate them with Search Console and an approved keyword research tool before investing in dedicated landing pages.

| Page or content | Primary query hypothesis | Supporting query hypotheses | Search intent | Content and claim guardrails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | local-first business management software | business management software for small businesses; browser-based business records; customer and sales management app | Discover and evaluate a business tool | Explain that data stays in browser storage and does not automatically cloud-sync. Do not promise team access or a cloud account. |
| `/` | subscription and customer management software | subscription tracking for small business; customer subscription tracker; service plan management | Compare tools for recurring service records | Describe tracking periods, status, expiry, and reminders only as implemented. Do not imply recurring billing or automated payment collection. |

| Brand navigation | XolveManager | XolveManager business management; XolveManager privacy; XolveManager backup | Find or verify the product | Use the configured product name consistently. Do not add unverified company/contact details. |

## Page targeting rules

- Give each indexable page one clear topic and a unique title, description, heading, and canonical URL.
- Keep support and draft policy pages noindex until their content and contact details are verified.
- Do not create near-duplicate location pages or pages that imply an unimplemented product feature.
- Do not publish pricing, savings, ratings, testimonials, customer counts, certifications, or market-leadership claims without evidence.
- Bengali-language keyword targets require a reviewed Bengali landing page and accurate localized product disclosures; do not generate translated pages that merely duplicate the English copy.
